Multi-UAV coordination, task allocation, formation control and airspace conflict resolution.

The Future Sky

A Timeline of Autonomous Flight

2020

GPS Drone Era

Remote piloting, waypoint navigation and increasingly capable imaging systems.

2025

AI Flight Era

Perception, automatic avoidance and onboard intelligence move into mainstream UAVs.

2030

Autonomous Sky Network

Many aircraft share data, missions and constraints across connected airspace.

2040

Intelligent Airspace

Aircraft, infrastructure and software coordinate continuously in a digital sky.

2050+

Sky as Infrastructure

Autonomous aerial networks become a normal layer of transportation and sensing.
